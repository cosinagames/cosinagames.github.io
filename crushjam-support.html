Crush Jam - Grinding It

Experience the ultimate in satisfying games with Crush Jam! This is more than just a color sort puzzle; it's a relaxing journey of tapping, crushing, and sorting that will soothe your mind.
If you're searching for a captivating logic puzzle game that doubles as a powerful brain teaser and stress-reliever, you've found it.
Best of all, it's a complete offline puzzle game you can play with just one hand!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I report a bug?
Please send us an email or contact us on Facebook with the following information:
Device model
iOS version
- Description of the issue
Screenshot or video (if possible)

2. My game progress is lost. What should I do?
If your game progress is missing or not syncing, please contact us with your device information and the approximate date you last played.

3. I can't make in-app purchases.
Please ensure:
- Your App Store payment method is valid
Your device is connected to the internet
Your region supports in-app purchases
If the issue continues, contact Apple Support or email us for help.

4. The game crashes or freezes.
Try the following steps:
- Restart the game
Restart your device
Ensure you are running the latest version of iOS
Make sure the app is updated to the latest version
If the problem persists, contact us with your device model and iOS version.
